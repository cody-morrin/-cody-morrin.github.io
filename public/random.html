How to play

Guess the word in 6 tries, like Wordle. Each guess must be a real five-letter word. Press Enter to submit.

After each guess the squares change color:

W
E
A
R
Y

Green: the letter is in the word and in the right spot. Yellow: it's in the word but in another spot. Grey: it isn't in the word.

Every square has a 15% chance of lying. When a square lies, it shows one of the other two colors instead. The keyboard only knows what the squares showed you.

Get the word right and that row is all green, honestly.

Daily is the same word for everyone, with a new one at midnight (your time). Random is a new word every time you load it.
